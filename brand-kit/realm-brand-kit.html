01 · Logo

Lockups and mark

On navy, use the verdigris R with a snow wordmark; this is the primary version. On white and in print, switch to the deep-verdigris R with an ink wordmark. Both greens are the accent tokens in the new palette.css.

04 · Favicon set

Right in light tabs and dark tabs

The SVG favicon changes with the browser theme: deep verdigris in light tabs, verdigris in dark tabs. PNG and ICO can't switch, so they carry the site in miniature: the verdigris R on a navy tile, which reads in both. The 16px versions are drawn by hand on the pixel grid.

05 · Website palette

A drop-in palette.css

Same file, same token names, so it replaces src/styles/palette.css with nothing else to touch. Only two tokens change in each theme: --c-accent and --c-accent-soft. In light mode the accent is a deeper verdigris so it stays readable on white. Navy surfaces, text, lines and the warn and danger colours are unchanged, and amber still reads clearly as a warning next to green.
