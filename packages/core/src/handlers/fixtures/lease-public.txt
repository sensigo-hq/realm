LEASE OF OFFICE PREMISES

THIS LEASE is made on 14 March 2025

BETWEEN

(1) TALLOWMERE ESTATES LIMITED, a company incorporated in Ireland (registered number FICT-0001), whose registered office is at 7 Halloran Row, Dublin 2, D02 QX61, Ireland (the "Landlord"); and

(2) NORTHWIND ANALYTICS LTD, a company incorporated in Ireland (registered number FICT-0002), whose registered office is at 41 Kestrel Street Lower, Dublin 2, D02 QX25, Ireland (the "Tenant").

BACKGROUND

(A) The Landlord is the owner of the building known as Quayside House, 3 Saltwater Quay, Dublin 2, D02 QT44, Ireland (the "Building").

(B) The Landlord has agreed to grant, and the Tenant has agreed to take, a lease of the Premises on the terms set out in this Lease.

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Lease the following words and expressions have the following meanings:

"Act" means the Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act 1980 and any statutory modification or re-enactment of it.

"Base Rate" means the main refinancing operations rate of the European Central Bank from time to time.

"Building" has the meaning given in Recital (A).

"Common Parts" means the entrance hall, stairs, lifts, corridors, toilets on common floors, the bin store, the bicycle store and all other parts of the Building which are from time to time provided by the Landlord for common use by the tenants and occupiers of the Building.

"Contractual Term" means the term of ten (10) years beginning on and including the Term Commencement Date and ending on and including the Term Expiry Date.

"Insured Risks" means fire, lightning, explosion, storm, flood, earthquake, aircraft and articles dropped from them, riot, civil commotion, malicious damage, impact by vehicles, subsidence, heave, landslip, burst pipes and such other risks as the Landlord from time to time reasonably decides to insure against.

"Interest" means interest at the rate of four per cent (4%) per annum above the Base Rate, calculated on a daily basis from the date on which the relevant payment was due until the date of actual payment, both before and after any judgment.

"Lettable Area" means the net internal area of the Premises measured in accordance with the RICS Code of Measuring Practice (6th edition), agreed between the parties at 412 square metres.

"Permitted Use" means use as offices within the meaning of Class 2 of Part 4 of Schedule 2 to the Planning and Development Regulations 2001, together with ancillary meeting and training rooms.

"Premises" means the whole of the third (3rd) floor of the Building, as shown edged red on Plan 1, including the internal plaster and other finishes to the structural walls and columns, the floor screed and finishes, the suspended ceilings and the voids above them, and all Landlord's fixtures and fittings within them.

"Rent" means the rent specified in clause 3.1, as varied from time to time under this Lease.

"Rent Commencement Date" means the date which is three (3) months after the Term Commencement Date.

"Review Date" means 1 May 2030.

"Service Charge" means the proportion of the Service Costs payable by the Tenant under Schedule 3.

"Term Commencement Date" means 1 May 2025.

"Term Expiry Date" means 30 April 2035.

1.2 References to a clause or Schedule are to a clause of or a Schedule to this Lease. Words importing one gender include all genders, and words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa.

1.3 Any obligation on the Tenant not to do something includes an obligation not to permit or suffer that thing to be done by another person.

2. GRANT

2.1 In consideration of the rents and the Tenant's covenants in this Lease, the Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for the Contractual Term, together with the rights set out in Schedule 1 and subject to the exceptions and reservations set out in Schedule 2.

2.2 The Tenant acknowledges that it has been advised by its solicitor to sign a renunciation of its rights under Part II of the Act, and that a deed of renunciation has been signed before the date of this Lease.

3. RENT

3.1 The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord an initial annual rent of forty-eight thousand euro (€48,000), exclusive of value added tax, subject to review under Schedule 4.

3.2 The Rent shall be paid by four equal instalments in advance on the usual quarter days, namely 1 February, 1 May, 1 August and 1 November in each year, by bank transfer to such account as the Landlord may nominate in writing.

3.3 The first payment of the Rent, being a proportionate sum from and including the Rent Commencement Date to (but excluding) the next quarter day, shall be paid on the Rent Commencement Date.

3.4 No Rent shall be payable in respect of the period from the Term Commencement Date to the Rent Commencement Date. During that period the Tenant shall nonetheless pay the Service Charge and the insurance rent.

3.5 If any Rent or other sum due under this Lease is not paid within fourteen (14) days of its due date, the Tenant shall pay Interest on it.

4. RENT DEPOSIT

4.1 On the date of this Lease the Tenant shall pay to the Landlord the sum of twenty-four thousand euro (€24,000) to be held as a rent deposit in accordance with the rent deposit deed of even date. The Landlord has agreed to accept this rent deposit in lieu of any guarantee of the Tenant's obligations, and no guarantor is a party to this Lease.

4.2 The Landlord may draw on the rent deposit to make good any failure by the Tenant to pay the Rent or any other sum due under this Lease, and the Tenant shall within ten (10) working days of written demand pay to the Landlord such sum as is needed to restore the rent deposit to its full amount.

5. SERVICE CHARGE

5.1 The Tenant shall pay the Service Charge in accordance with Schedule 3.

5.2 The Service Charge payable in respect of any service charge year shall not exceed nine thousand two hundred euro (€9,200), save to the extent that any increase is caused by a change in law or by works required to comply with any statutory requirement.

6. INSURANCE

6.1 The Landlord shall insure the Building (excluding any tenant's fixtures and fittings) against the Insured Risks for its full reinstatement cost, and shall use reasonable endeavours to procure that the insurer waives its rights of subrogation against the Tenant.

6.2 The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord on demand, as insurance rent, a fair proportion of the cost of the insurance effected under clause 6.1, together with the cost of a professional valuation of the Building for insurance purposes once every three years.

6.3 The Tenant shall insure its own fixtures, fittings and contents and shall maintain public liability insurance in respect of its use of the Premises with a limit of indemnity of not less than six million five hundred thousand euro (€6,500,000) for any one occurrence.

7. TENANT'S COVENANTS

7.1 Repair. The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good and substantial repair and condition, damage by the Insured Risks excepted. The Tenant shall not be responsible for repairs to the structure, roof, foundations or exterior of the Building, which remain the responsibility of the Landlord at the cost of the Service Charge.

7.2 Decoration. The Tenant shall decorate the inside of the Premises in every fifth year of the Contractual Term and in the last three months of the Contractual Term (however it ends).

7.3 Use. The Tenant shall not use the Premises other than for the Permitted Use.

7.4 Alterations. The Tenant shall not make any structural alterations to the Premises. The Tenant may install and remove internal demountable partitioning with the Landlord's consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

7.5 Alienation. The Tenant shall not assign, underlet, charge or part with possession of the whole or any part of the Premises, save that the Tenant may assign the whole of the Premises with the Landlord's consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

7.6 Statutory obligations. The Tenant shall comply with all laws relating to the Premises and to its use of them, including all requirements of the fire authority.

7.7 Yield up. At the end of the term, the Tenant shall yield up the Premises in the repair and condition required by this Lease, having removed all tenant's fixtures, fittings and signage and made good any damage caused by their removal.

8. LANDLORD'S COVENANTS

8.1 Quiet enjoyment. The Landlord covenants that the Tenant may peaceably hold and enjoy the Premises during the term without any interruption by the Landlord or any person lawfully claiming under or in trust for the Landlord.

8.2 Services. Subject to payment of the Service Charge, the Landlord shall use reasonable endeavours to provide the services set out in Part 2 of Schedule 3.

9. TENANT'S BREAK OPTION

9.1 The Tenant may terminate this Lease on 30 April 2030 (the "Break Date") by giving the Landlord not less than six (6) months' prior written notice.

9.2 A break notice served by the Tenant shall take effect only if, on the Break Date, the Tenant has paid all Rent due under this Lease and gives vacant possession of the Premises.

9.3 Following termination under this clause, the Landlord shall refund to the Tenant any Rent, Service Charge or insurance rent paid in respect of any period after the Break Date.

10. FORFEITURE

10.1 If any Rent is unpaid twenty-one (21) days after becoming payable (whether formally demanded or not), or if the Tenant is in breach of any covenant in this Lease, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent, the Landlord may re-enter the Premises (or any part of them) at any time afterwards, and the term shall then end, without prejudice to any right of the Landlord in respect of any breach of covenant.

11. NOTICES

11.1 Any notice given under this Lease shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand or sent by pre-paid registered post.

11.2 Notices to the Landlord shall be sent to its asset management office at 22 Ashgrove Square South, Dublin 2, D02 QK53, Ireland, marked for the attention of the Head of Asset Management, or to such other address as the Landlord may notify to the Tenant in writing.

11.3 Notices to the Tenant shall be sent to the Premises.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 This Lease and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of Ireland.

12.2 The courts of Ireland shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Lease.

SCHEDULE 1 — RIGHTS GRANTED

1. A right for the Tenant and persons authorised by it to pass on foot over the Common Parts for the purposes of access to and egress from the Premises.

2. A right to use the lifts in the Building, subject to the Landlord's right to suspend their use for maintenance.

3. A right to park bicycles in the bicycle store in common with other occupiers.

4. A right to display the Tenant's name on the directory board in the entrance hall, in a form approved by the Landlord.

SCHEDULE 2 — EXCEPTIONS AND RESERVATIONS

1. Rights of support and protection for the remainder of the Building.

2. The right for the Landlord to enter the Premises on not less than forty-eight (48) hours' notice (except in an emergency) to inspect them, to carry out repairs to the Building and to exercise the Landlord's other rights.

3. The right to erect scaffolding for the purpose of repairing or cleaning the Building.

SCHEDULE 3 — SERVICE CHARGE

Part 1 — Calculation

1. The Tenant's proportion of the Service Costs is the proportion which the Lettable Area bears to the total lettable area of the Building, being 18.5 per cent at the date of this Lease.

2. The service charge year runs from 1 January to 31 December. The Landlord shall provide an estimate of the Service Costs before the start of each service charge year, and the Tenant shall pay the Service Charge on account by quarterly instalments on the quarter days.

Part 2 — Services

1. Cleaning, lighting, heating and maintaining the Common Parts.

2. Maintaining and repairing the structure, roof, foundations and exterior of the Building, and the lifts and the mechanical and electrical plant.

3. Providing reception and security services during business hours.

SCHEDULE 4 — RENT REVIEW

1. On the Review Date, the Rent shall be reviewed to the higher of (a) the Rent payable immediately before the Review Date and (b) the open market rent of the Premises at the Review Date, assessed on the assumptions and disregards set out in this Schedule. The Rent shall not be reduced on review.

2. If the Landlord and the Tenant have not agreed the open market rent three (3) months before the Review Date, either party may refer its determination to an independent chartered surveyor acting as an expert, appointed by agreement or, failing agreement, by the President of the Society of Chartered Surveyors Ireland.

SIGNED AND DELIVERED as a deed by NORTHWIND ANALYTICS LTD, acting by a director in the presence of a witness, on 14 March 2025.
